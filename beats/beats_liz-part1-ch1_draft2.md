# Beat Plan — "liz-part1-ch1" draft 2 (Elizabeth, 1st person, present tense)

Redo per author correction: fully ordinary chapter, no alley/fight/stranger, no supernatural content, plain teenage diction throughout. Same basic shape as the superseded draft (morning with Sam, errand into town) but purely mundane start to finish, ending on an ordinary note rather than anything shaken.

1. Kitchen, morning. Elizabeth makes coffee. Small competitive back-and-forth with Sam over who makes it better / whose turn it is — plain, dry banter, nothing writerly. Establishes warm caretaker dynamic and her habit of being fine on the surface (doesn't mention anything she's carrying, there's nothing dramatic to mention — just ordinary low-key tiredness she waves off).
2. Grocery list / errand talk. Sam reads off what they need (eggs, her pharmacy refill, a battery for the clock radio). She needles him lightly about signing up for fall classes; he deflects, changes subject back to the list. Kept light — no fight, no resolution, just texture of the standing tension between them.
3. He offers to come with her, she turns him down, normal back-and-forth, no weight to it.
4. She walks to town — square/park (fountain, benches, ring road), nothing happens, just ordinary town texture (someone she sort of knows, weather, the usual). Picks up her prescription at the pharmacy — short ordinary transaction.
5. Grocery store for eggs and the battery. Small mundane decision (brand of eggs, forgets something small and has to double back or decides not to bother) — keeps the "competitive/notices things" trait in play without inventing drama.
6. Walks home. Nothing happens. Thinks about ordinary things — what's for dinner, something Sam said, the heat.
7. Home. Hands off the bag, small final exchange with Sam, something plain and warm. Chapter ends on an ordinary, unresolved, low-stakes note — no shake-up, no cliffhanger.

Notes:
- No named new characters beyond Sam (and maybe a passing, unnamed neighbor/clerk if needed for texture — no dialogue of substance from them, nothing supernatural, nothing suspicious).
- No hair/eye color description for anyone.
- No elapsed-time markers.
- Keep figures of speech at or under 3 total (dialogue + narration), and only if they clear all four tests — default to none/plain phrasing given the plain-diction mandate for Elizabeth specifically.
- No standalone "said" tags — attach to action/reaction beats.
