**Reset 2026-09-11.** The 69-chapter outline that used to fill this file was built around the old character/bond system and is retired along with it (see `notes/creative-partner-session.md` for the full rework). Chapters "Breach" through "Dress" have been scratched entirely.

**This file remains the only place reading order lives.** No chapter file, bible entry, or note carries a number — a title is a stable identifier that never changes, but its *position* is only ever recorded here, as this list's own top-to-bottom order.

**New workflow (2026-09-11):** the author writes a paragraph or two describing what happens in a chapter (not a full unit-plan entry). The Writer agent drafts from that, seeing ONLY the immediately previous chapter — never the rest of the book, never the full bible. The author reviews the draft, sends it to the revision team (Continuity Checker, Story Integrity), and the Director locks the chapter once the revision team is satisfied. Minor lingering issues wait for the author's own final pass once the whole book is done.
