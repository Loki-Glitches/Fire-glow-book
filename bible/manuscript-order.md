# Manuscript Order — The Single Source of Truth for Sequence

**Reset 2026-09-11.** The 69-chapter outline that used to fill this file was built around the old character/bond system and is retired along with it (see `notes/creative-partner-session.md` for the full rework). Chapters "Breach" through "Dress" have been scratched entirely.

**This file remains the only place reading order lives.** No chapter file, bible entry, or note carries a number — a title is a stable identifier that never changes, but its *position* is only ever recorded here, as this list's own top-to-bottom order.

**New workflow (2026-09-11, narrowed further same day):** the author writes a paragraph or two describing what happens in a chapter (not a full unit-plan entry). The Writer agent drafts from that, seeing ONLY the author's paragraph plus `notes/character-synopsis.md` — never the previous chapter's text, never the rest of the book, never the full bible. The author reviews the draft, sends it to the revision team (Continuity Checker, Story Integrity), and the Director locks the chapter once the revision team is satisfied. Minor lingering issues wait for the author's own final pass once the whole book is done.

2. **[Hell](../chapters/hell.md)** — Lucifer — drafted and locked (2026-09-11), author's own hand-edited final text. Sebastian's report, the throne room/Tree of Knowledge, Eve's escape and Lucifer's failed catch, Lilith installed as regent, God's terms for Earth (no divinity, commandment limits, Azrael accompanying him). No Revision pass run — author judged it unnecessary since it's the only Lucifer chapter so far, nothing yet to check it against.

3. **[Breakfast](../chapters/breakfast.md)** — Elizabeth — drafted and locked (2026-09-11). Elizabeth comes downstairs (floral dress), smells Sam's cooking, sits at the kitchen table; Sam notices the cut on her head from the alarm/fall incident in "Morning," she deflects it as losing a fight with the table; small talk about college prep; she notices a new London postcard on the fridge, doesn't press for more than what's on it; she finishes eating first, offers to grocery-shop, Sam asks for the usual plus a chocolate croissant from the cafe she's going to with Sophie ("Soph") — he already knows her routine. She leaves. No Revision pass run — author reviewed and confirmed as-is.

4. **[Why Did It Have to Be Apples](../chapters/why-did-it-have-to-be-apples.md)** — Lucifer — drafted, awaiting author review. Lucifer crashes out of the portal into an orchard, sees Azrael already waiting, plows through two apple trees. He and Azrael argue (rules already established in "Hell" — this is friction over Lucifer's pattern of forcing Azrael into new roles, not a first-time rules explainer). James and Claire arrive, notice the wrecked trees, get deflected ("We don't know"), invite the brothers to a picnic lunch. Azrael fields most of the background questions; Lucifer, bound by the Divine Truth mechanic, has to honestly name who they're looking for — Eve — when asked directly. James proposes to Claire with a cheap ring; Lucifer swaps it for a better one via sleight-of-hand (not transfiguration — has to read as a magic trick to onlookers, not visible power). Lucifer and Azrael debate and decide to ask James and Claire for help finding Eve (unknown arrival time, but they'll recognize her on sight — she'll look "out of the times"). Elizabeth comes up; the brothers ask where to find her. Before leaving, Lucifer quietly repairs the tree he shattered. Chapter ends as they leave.

5. **My Usual** — Elizabeth — in progress. Elizabeth grocery shops (general groceries, money isn't tight, one-off characters greet her by name). At checkout she notices two strangers through the window — describes clothing and position, not faces (this is the reader's first glimpse of Lucifer/Adrian and Azrael/Tristan from Elizabeth's side, unrecognized). She checks out, leaves, still half-watching them. Sophie startles her; they load groceries into Sophie's car, Elizabeth returns the cart. They drive to the cafe — Sophie gets iced coffee and a cookie, Elizabeth gets an iced fruity drink and a croissant, plus an iced coffee for Sam. Seating area, girl talk. They leave, and as Elizabeth steps out the door, Sophie runs into Azrael. Chapter ends there.

6. **Introductions Are In Order** — Lucifer — in progress. Opens a few minutes before "My Usual" ends: Lucifer and Azrael walking through town, brainstorming how to find Eve faster; Azrael warns Lucifer against using his power the way he did at the orchard. Sophie walks out of the coffee house and collides with Azrael (the moment "My Usual" ends on); he helps clean up and apologizes, she shrugs it off. The four sit on facing benches and introduce themselves — Lucifer and Azrael clock it when Elizabeth introduces herself last. They mention the orchard incident and that James and Claire pointed them toward Elizabeth. Elizabeth asks what they need help with; they give Eve's name and the same vague "out of the times" description they gave James and Claire. Sophie remarks on the coincidence of how they all met. Elizabeth invites them to walk together. Conversation splits: Elizabeth quizzes Azrael on the Eve search (work questions), Sophie interrogates Lucifer about his personal life (he keeps pausing, avoiding detail). They swap: Elizabeth and Lucifer, Azrael and Sophie. Elizabeth asks Lucifer how they know Eve; he redirects to Tristan. Elizabeth and Sophie head back to the car and leave.

7. **Rundown** — Elizabeth — assigned to Writer. Opens right after "Introductions Are In Order": Sophie drives Elizabeth home; in the car Elizabeth wonders how they'll help find Eve with no way to reach the brothers, worried about the missing woman, not Adrian. They unload groceries at Elizabeth and Sam's house. Inside, Sam takes the melted iced coffee and croissant Elizabeth carried, kisses Sophie hello. Elizabeth passes on Mr. Higgins's invoice message; Sam says "good," done. Sam goes to make lunch; Sophie stays. Alone in the living room, Elizabeth and Sophie talk about the missing woman; Elizabeth gets a brief head twinge mid-conversation, blames it on heat/tiredness. Elizabeth says she can't stand Adrian's personality and would rather research with Tristan. Sophie mentions, as an odd detail, that Adrian said "I don't have a father" and crushed an apple in his fist — Elizabeth registers it and dismisses it, no softening. Sophie offers to help; they realize they have no way to contact the brothers and settle on going to the park to see if they turn up. Sam comes back in, catches part of it, gets protective; Elizabeth says they weren't aggressive but concedes he'll be wary anyway. Ends there.
