# GABRIEL — Character Profile

The biblical archangel Gabriel: God's messenger. One of Lucifer's angelic siblings. On Earth he carries out God's will directly.

**He is the angel who typically interacts physically with the human world**, creating and arranging real things within human systems (records, identities, accounts, documents) as God directs. Other angels' work on Earth is normally immaterial.

**Exceptions:** Lucifer and Azrael act physically on Earth because they're on a special task (hunting Eve / containing Lucifer). Lucifer is also permitted regardless of task, because of his free will (see `bible/lucifer_character_profile.md` § FORM & SOUL).

**First on-page effect:** "Errands." When Lucifer opens a bank account as "Adrian Black," one already exists with his identity set up and no money in it; Lucifer knows at once it was Gabriel. Gabriel himself has not appeared on the page.

**Open:** whether Gabriel is the sibling currently covering Azrael's death-duty; whether Azrael knew about the account; how the account already used "Black," a name Lucifer chose that same day.
