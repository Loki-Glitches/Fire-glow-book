# Chapter 1 — Elizabeth

The alarm roared on Elizabeth’s nightstand — Beep! Beep! Beep! — forcing her to wake up groggy and dazed.

“Five more minutes,” she declared softly as she swung her hand in a windmill motion to hit the snooze button. To her embarrassment, she missed entirely; her hand slapped hard against the bedside table, sending a jolt that sent tingles to all the nerves in her hand and wrist.

"Ow!" she screamed into her pillow, clutching her hand to ease the sting as the alarm continued to shriek even louder, mocking her attempts to wake up.

With one last attempt, she threw her arm at the clock again, finally silencing it, only to lose her balance and tumble out of bed, her forehead slamming against the nightstand with a painful thud, sending her head first onto the cold, hard floor.

She lay in the position for what seemed like an eternity, still tangled in the sheets, trying to summon the strength to wake up. The silence began to settle in, but she couldn't manage to get her body to cooperate.

“Ow,” she mumbled again in short sobs. She stayed there for a long moment, the dark wood of the floor pressing into her cheek, her bedraggled hair splayed out like a mess of forgotten thoughts. Dust clung to the sleeve of her pajamas.

The sunlight filtered softly through the parted curtains, casting faint shadows on the floor and blinding her as she lay there in silence.

As the sunlight reached her eyes, her head began to ring sharply. Attempting to quiet the noise, she massaged her temple, but to no avail, wincing at the pressure. After a moment, she groaned, pushing herself up onto her knees.

When she finally stood up, the world spun around her violently, her vision turning blurry for a few seconds before clearing.

Elizabeth steadied herself and walked slowly toward the full-length mirror on her closet door. Reaching over to her dresser, she compared several outfits and tossed the bad ones onto the bed in a heap. Once she finally decided on one, she placed it neatly on the bed and grabbed the others from the bed, carefully folded them, and put them away in the drawers.

After double-checking the outfit she chose, she walked toward the bathroom to shower, letting out a long sigh. She closed her eyes gently, already dreading the day of drama, embarrassment, and chaos that was in store for her.
