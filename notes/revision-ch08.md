## CRAFT NOTES

- **POV correction made during drafting, important for future chapters:** my first draft rendered Tristan and Lucifer's telepathic bond-challenge exchange directly, in italics, the way Ch.4 does it. That's wrong here — Ch.4 is Lucifer's own POV chapter, so his bond traffic is fair game; Ch.8 is strict Elizabeth POV, and she has no access to a silent telepathic exchange between two other people. Rewrote every instance as an external tell she can actually observe (a cut glance, an unreadable jaw, an unexplained pause) without revealing content. **This is the pattern for any future non-Lucifer-POV chapter that needs to show the standing "Tristan always challenges the bonds" rule** — the challenge still happens in-universe, the reader just doesn't get the words unless the POV character is Lucifer.
- Caught and fixed a second POV slip in the same pass: several lines flatly asserted Lucifer's or Tristan's interior state as narrated fact ("which surprised him," "he could feel him not saying it," "he hadn't planned to ask it") — none of which Elizabeth can know. Rewrote all of them as her external observations/inferences instead.
- Caught five separate word-reuse simile violations under the newly-locked test (§ "No Mechanism Similes," `bible/style-guide.md`) during self-review — "quiet...quiet," "watching...watched," "said...said" (twice), "filed...filed," "looked...looked," "turning...turned." All rewritten to avoid the repeated root. Good real-world proof the new test catches things that "feel fine" on a first pass.
- No physical description of Adrian, Tristan, or the kids at the park.
- Narration uses "Adrian," never "Lucifer," throughout — Elizabeth's POV, she doesn't know his real name yet. Caught one slip during drafting and fixed it.
