## Scope of this pass
- This run is purely to get the plot itself down. The author expects to add important plot elements later — this is a skeleton pass, not a final-prose pass.
- **"Morning" (the author's own hand-written first chapter) stays exactly as-is.** Every other existing chapter ("Breach" through "Dress") is being scratched — the character rework below makes them non-canonical.

## New workflow (separate from the concept changes below, but decided in the same conversation)
- Author writes a paragraph or two describing what happens in a chapter (not full unit-plan-style beats, not full prose).
- Writer agent (being reinstated) drafts from that — and is NOT allowed to see the whole book, only the immediately previous chapter. No full bible context dump to the Writer.
- Author reviews the draft, sends it to the revision/review agents.
- Once the revision team is satisfied, Director locks the chapter.
- Minor lingering issues get fixed by the author in a final pass once the whole book is done — don't block a chapter over something small.

## Lucifer — reworked characterization
- **Inverted emotional arc:** starts with bad/negative emotions only (lashes out at small things, snide, sarcastic, angry, annoyed) and improves as the book progresses — the opposite of the old Numbness→Calm→Beauty/Desire→... ladder, which assumed he starts emotionally flat and unlocks positive emotion progressively. That whole ladder system needs rethinking against this new direction.
- **Has power from the start.** Not gradually unlocked by bond tier. The constraint on using it is behavioral/secrecy-based, not tier-gated.
- **Strict orders: never reveal his divinity himself.** Exception: if a human arrives at the truth independently (via belief or evidence), that's allowed to stand/be confirmed — he doesn't have to hide it retroactively once someone's genuinely figured it out on their own.
- **Power-use rule: allowed to use power as long as no human physically witnesses it.** If he has to use power where someone might see, he plays it off as stage magic/trickery rather than admit it's real.
- **Strict orders not to tell anyone why he's here** (his actual mission/purpose stays secret regardless of divinity disclosure).
- **Motivation:** hates Earth specifically (cast out of it — note: reconcile with existing lore that the Fall was being cast out of Heaven; author's phrasing suggests Earth/Eden banishment is also part of it now, needs clarifying with the author). Does NOT resent God — just dislikes humans generally. Willing to use them instrumentally: deals, twisting truth, whatever it takes.
- **Still commandment-bound** (cannot break them) **but exploits their broadness** — deliberate, knowing loophole use, more manipulative in framing than the old "technically true, careful" version.
- **Relationship with Elizabeth is purely transactional at first.** She genuinely hates him initially.
- Aliases/human names stay in use regardless of what's known — not yet clear if this changes the existing Adrian/Tristan alias mechanic or just confirms it continues.

## Elizabeth — reworked characterization
- Genuinely hates Lucifer at first, but tries to get to know him anyway.
- **Core trait: drawn to things she perceives as broken.** She can tell he's lashing out at something and wants to figure out what. This is her stated reason for persisting despite hating him — needs to be established as a defining character trait, not just a one-off.
- Once she independently figures out what he is, he's free to use power openly around her (ties to the power-use rule above).
- Constant conflict/friction with Lucifer — he's snide/sarcastic/angry, she gets mad back, but she can't be killed by him, so it stays argument, not danger.

## Azrael/Tristan — reworked role
- **No longer holds the "death duty"** (absorbing the pain of every human death) — that's been reassigned to a different angel, temporarily, for the duration of this arc. (New angel character not yet named/detailed.)
- **New job: contain Lucifer.** Less "evaluate whether he's fit to bond," more active management/restraint of a volatile, power-abusing Lucifer.
- **Must be present in every Lucifer scene until Elizabeth knows the truth** — a hard scene-construction rule while she's still in the dark.
- **Physically fights Lucifer in the book** — author's note: the previous tension between them "was too tame before." Tristan can genuinely hurt Lucifer, up to the point of death — neither of them can actually die, they heal within 24 hours no matter how bad the injury. They can cripple each other. Fights happen mainly at the house or outside of town.

## New setting
- **A shared home base: a vacant mansion at the top of the town hill**, which Lucifer buys. Presumably where Lucifer and Azrael are based, and likely where some of the Tristan/Lucifer fights happen.

## Bond system — simplified
- **Keeping:** Travel Bond only, same tether/range mechanic as before (~20ft, pulled to whichever bonded person he's near, etc. — needs confirming against the old `bible/bond_system_reference.md` version, since the range mechanic may or may not still apply the same way now that power isn't gated by bond tier). He has to be attached to either Azrael or Elizabeth once she figures him out.
- **Removing entirely:** Power Bond and Full Anchor. Power is no longer gated by relationship/bond progression at all — see "has power from the start" above.
