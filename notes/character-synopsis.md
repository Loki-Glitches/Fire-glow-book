- **The town green / park.** It's a town-square plaza, not an open field. Stores line all four outer sides, facing in. A ring road runs around the plaza between the stores and the central green. A stop sign sits at each of the four outer corners. Two concentric pedestrian paths: one along the storefronts (outside the ring road), the other circling the central fountain (linking the benches).
