## DIRECTOR FIXES (2026-09-04)

The writer sub-agent's draft was strong and hit every beat, but I made three small craft corrections before passing it:

1. **Cut an overwritten/hedge-adjacent clause on the opening beat.** "Tristan was the one who moved first — stepping back from where he'd been standing near the end of the bench, like he'd finally found a long enough gap in the quiet to fit through." The "like he'd finally found..." clause explains the meaning of the action instead of just stating it — exactly the pattern the style guide's "No Overwritten Posture/Action" rule targets. Cut to the plain action.
2. **Cut a mechanism simile.** "He said it the way someone describes weather, no weight added anywhere" matches the banned "the way [X] does [Y]" pattern — it makes the reader construct an unrelated scenario (someone describing weather) and map it back onto the scene. Replaced with "He said it plainly, no weight added anywhere."
3. **Removed a line that presses on the Eve deflection.** The draft had Elizabeth respond to "She came later" with "That's it? 'She came later'?" before letting it go. That's a press, however mild — the unit plan's Must NOT is explicit that she moves on *without* pressing, and this is the first instance of a pattern later chapters are told to check against, so it needed to be clean. Cut the pushback line; she now goes straight from his non-answer to registering it internally and moving on, matching the established Ch.8 precedent for how she handles a deflection (a beat of waiting, then letting it go, no verbal follow-up demanding more).
