**Summary, short form (see the docs above for the real detail, this is not a substitute):**
- Lucifer has power from the start of the book — no longer gated by bond progression. Constrained by secrecy orders instead: never self-disclose his divinity (unless a human figures it out independently), never reveal his mission, never use power where a human can witness it (passes it off as stage magic if he must).
- His baseline personality is volatile — snide, sarcastic, angry, lashes out at small things — softening in small, informal, unnamed beats over the book, with real acceleration once he falls for Elizabeth. The old named emotional ladder is retired with no formal replacement.
- He's the original angel — God gave him his form, every other angel is patterned from his image (this is also the in-universe reason he can bond across both alignments, an existing fact this reinforces rather than contradicts). Not God's offspring — God's most devoted worshipper, because God could create existence from nothing and no angel could. His own gift was smaller-scale creation (art, music) — the thing God loved him for. He paints privately from early in the book; Elizabeth discovers it before she knows the truth about him, and it's her first real evidence he's hurt rather than simply hostile. He sings later, only after she knows, only because she pushes him into it.
- He was banished from Eden (now relocated to Heaven, per existing cosmology) and is separately barred from Heaven by his own duties as Hell's warden — both existing facts, reframed to explain his resentment of Earth specifically. Does not resent God.
- Elizabeth genuinely hates him at first. She stays in his orbit because of a new core trait: she's drawn to things she perceives as broken, and can tell he's lashing out at something. Constant arguing/friction is their default mode — he can't kill her, so there's heat but no real danger.
- Azrael's usual death-duty (absorbing the pain of every human death) is temporarily reassigned to a different, unnamed angel. His new job is to contain Lucifer, not evaluate his fitness to bond. He must be present in every Lucifer scene until Elizabeth knows the truth. He and Lucifer physically fight over the course of the book — can genuinely cripple or nearly kill each other, but neither can actually die; both heal within 24 hours. Fights happen mainly at the shared mansion or outside of town.
- Bond system: Travel Bond is the only tier that survives (same ~20ft tether/range mechanic as before), now purely a proximity mechanic, not a power gate. Power Bond and Full Anchor are cut entirely. Once Elizabeth knows the truth, Lucifer has to stay attached to either her or Azrael.
- New setting: a vacant mansion at the top of the town hill, which Lucifer buys — shared home base for him and Azrael.

## 3. The Pipeline (reworked twice in one day — this is the current, third version)

**Writer is reinstated (2026-09-11), superseding the "author writes every chapter themselves" rule from earlier the same day.** The author doesn't have time to write the full manuscript alongside college — agents draft again, but under a much narrower context rule than the original pipeline ever used.

**New chapter-planning workflow:** the author writes a paragraph or two describing what happens in a chapter — NOT a full unit-plan entry, not full prose. The Writer agent drafts the actual chapter from that paragraph. **Writer is only allowed to see the immediately previous chapter — never the rest of the book, never the full bible, never a unit-plan-style outline.** This is a hard, deliberate restriction, not an oversight — keep Writer's context to exactly: the author's paragraph for this chapter + the previous chapter's text, nothing else. The author reviews the draft, sends it to the revision team (Continuity Checker, Story Integrity). Once the revision team is satisfied, the Director locks the chapter. Minor lingering issues wait for the author's own final pass once the whole book is done — don't block a chapter over something small.

**No agent rewrites a chapter based on review findings.** Continuity Checker and Story Integrity report findings only; it's the author's (or, per their direction, Writer's own revision pass's) job to act on them, not an automatic Director edit.

**Creative Partner is active**, reinstated for ongoing mid-project brainstorming — calibrated to push back, but the author's decision always stands; an override becomes a new mechanic, not a blocked conflict, unless it fully contradicts something already locked. This is how the 2026-09-11 rework itself was built — see `notes/creative-partner-session.md`.

Check `.claude/agents/agent-generic-director.md`'s Standing Rule for the exact current agent roster before assuming any specific agent is or isn't active — it's changed twice in one day already and may change again.
