## 3. Locked Canon (2026-09-11 rework + since)

Full detail lives in the companion bible docs listed in § 1; this is the load-bearing summary.

- **Lucifer has power from the start of the book.** Not bond-gated. Constrained instead by standing orders: never self-disclose his own divinity (exception: if a human independently figures it out, that's allowed to stand), never reveal his mission, never use power where a human can witness it (passes it off as a magic trick if he must). — *`bible/lucifer_character_profile.md` § "Power & Secrecy"*
- **His baseline personality is volatile** — snide, sarcastic, angry, lashes out at small things — softening in small, informal, unnamed beats over the book, with real acceleration once he falls for Elizabeth.
- **He is the original angel** — God gave him his form; every other angel is patterned from his image. Not God's offspring — God's most devoted worshipper. His own gift was smaller-scale creation (art, music).
- **God is NOT Lucifer's father.** Their bond reads closer to siblings.
- **He paints privately from early in the book; sings later, only after she knows, only because she pushes him into it. Neither beat has happened on the page yet** as of "Ambush."
- **Elizabeth genuinely hates him at first. This is no longer a developing thread — it's now a stated, crystallized fact as of "Ambush"** ("I really don't like him," unchallenged by Sophie). Constant arguing/friction remains their default mode; he can't kill her, so there's heat but no real danger.
- **Azrael's usual death-duty is temporarily reassigned** to a different, unnamed angel (still not named — § 7). His job is to contain Lucifer. Present in every Lucifer scene until Elizabeth knows the truth. He and Lucifer can physically hurt each other badly but neither can die; both heal within 24 hours, consistent with "No Place Like Home"'s single-shove injury (Lucifer's back) and both "Errands" and "Ambush" tracking that injury's lingering limp.
- **Bond system: Travel Bond is the only tier that survives** (~20ft proximity mechanic, not a power gate). Power Bond and Full Anchor are cut entirely.
- **Names:** Lucifer → Adrian; Azrael → Tristan. Narration uses true names regardless of scene; only spoken self-introduction or human address uses the alias. Azrael privately calls Lucifer "Lucy"; God calls him "Luc."
- **Eve is the antagonist, absent from the page until Act Three.**

### 2026-10-02 additions (`bible/lucifer_character_profile.md` §§ FORM & SOUL / POWER SCOPE, and `bible/gabriel_character_profile.md`) — unchanged this pass, restated for completeness

- **FORM & SOUL.** Lucifer's **soul is uncreated** — it existed before God found him, is entirely his own, and is **permanently unalterable: beyond God's power to change or take, unconditionally.** No other angel shares this. His **form**, by contrast, **was a gift from God** — a vessel to use, not a claim of authorship or ownership — and **because it was given, it can be taken back.** The form is also a deliberate limiter on his divine power (see POWER SCOPE). **Because his soul was never God's work, Lucifer has the greatest free will of any angel** — the stated reason he, unlike other angels, is permitted to act physically on Earth regardless of his assigned task (ties to Gabriel, below).
- **Purity, restated under the new cosmology.** In his given form Lucifer **cannot sin, physically and literally** — not a temptation resisted, an actual impossibility. **Never write him straining against it.** The underlying cosmology (sin becoming possible only in his full, outside-body/outside-time state, which he never enters) is explicitly **narratively inert, reference-only, not a plot lever.**
- **POWER SCOPE.** Lucifer's full power is **"almost on the same level as God"** — he can do anything except create from nothing. **That full power exists only outside his body, outside time and space.** **Inside his given form (where he always is on the page), God has built in deliberate limits.** Still enormously powerful within those limits, but far below full scope — **this is explicitly why Azrael can genuinely hurt him in a fight and why he can be caught off guard.** Power is still not knowledge: does not make him all-knowing (see "He Is Not All-Knowing," unchanged).
- **General human-desire knowledge, not individual mind-reading.** As ruler of Hell, Lucifer has institutional knowledge of worldly human desire in general — never extends to reading what a specific individual wants or is thinking.
- **"Deals aren't sins."** Bribes and similar transactions are deals, not sins. First demonstrated on-page in "Errands" (the $5,000 bribe to Kevin Marsh).
- **Gabriel (`bible/gabriel_character_profile.md`).** The biblical archangel, one of Lucifer's angelic siblings, God's messenger. **He is the angel who typically handles physical arrangements in the human world on God's behalf.** Lucifer and Azrael are exceptions because of their special task; Lucifer is independently exempt regardless of task because of his free will. **First on-page effect, not yet a physical appearance:** in "Errands," Lucifer discovers a bank account already set up under "Adrian Black," empty, with no creation date, and immediately knows it was Gabriel. **Still open** (§ 7): whether Gabriel covers Azrael's reassigned death-duty; whether Azrael knew about the account in advance; how the account could already carry "Black," a surname Lucifer chose that same day.
- **Two new named minor characters, one-off, "Errands":** Kevin Marsh (phone-shop clerk) and Denise Harlow (bank clerk, First Link Banking).

## 4. Standing Character-Voice Rules (apply to every chapter, not just the one that introduced them)

These live in `notes/character-synopsis.md` under "Standing habits" specifically so Writer — who never reads the bible — still sees them. Treat this section as load-bearing for any new chapter brief.

1. **Elizabeth/Lucifer friction is mandatory, not chemistry.** His caustic baseline is aimed at her as much as anyone; friction/open arguing is their default mode from the very first exchange; she stays in his orbit because she reads him as broken, not because she likes him yet. No warmth/attraction registering in his interiority toward her; no comfortable silences between them specifically. **As of "Ambush," this friction has an explicit floor: she has stated outright that she really doesn't like him — treat this as the baseline going forward, not a feeling still being established.**
2. **Elizabeth has no lie-detection ability at all (correction fully closed as of "Ambush" — see § 3).** She asks pointed questions and watches for inconsistencies — an ordinary skeptical-questioning habit, a skill, not a perception power. All six locations, including `.claude/agents/agent-generic-writer.md`, now carry the corrected wording.
3. **Not every character reads people.** Only **Elizabeth** (skeptical-questioning habit, per #2 above — not perceptiveness) and **Lucifer/Azrael reading each other** (both directions, six thousand years of proximity) get any analytical read on others. Nobody else — Sophie, James, Claire, Sam, any extra — catches subtext or reads a tell. **Revision flagged one near-miss on this rule in "Ambush"** (a line giving Elizabeth an inferred read on what a conversation "cost" Adrian emotionally) — the author hand-edited the line afterward (now "the whole conversation seeming to have exhausted him") rather than cutting the inference outright; treat this specific line as an author-accepted judgment call, not a reopened rule.
4. **Lucifer eats apples constantly, as a habit** — ordinary apples, distinct from the (closed) Tree of Knowledge fruit thread. Bored, frustrated, or simply in the mood are all equally valid reasons. He cannot set one down once picked up — must eat or destroy it. **"Errands" is a deliberate, author-specified exception with no apple at all** — not a change to the standing habit. **"Ambush" resumes it explicitly** — he reaches for one mid-confrontation, specifically so it sets up the chapter's later "apple gimmick" exchange (he admits he likes the taste and it comforts him; Elizabeth calls it stress eating; he agrees).
5. **No similes, no metaphors in narration** — zero exceptions in narration; dialogue is exempt. A deliberate floor, not permanent; no retroactive sweep planned until the end of the manuscript. **"Ambush" passed this cleanly, per Revision.**
6. **No explicit physical description** of hair color or eye color, for any character, ever.
7. **Lucifer is not all-knowing.** Unaffected by the POWER SCOPE cosmology (§ 3) — raw power is explicitly not knowledge.
8. **Divine/telepathic speech formatting:** italics, no quotation marks, for God's voice and any telepathic bond traffic.
9. **The Sky-Glance:** Lucifer's silent upward glance at God's too-convenient timing — genuine annoyance, not shared amusement, Lucifer's POV only. (Not used in "Ambush" — Elizabeth POV, no access.)
10. **Lucifer cannot sin or lie.** Not a temptation he resists — physically impossible inside his given form. Never dramatize him straining against it. **"Ambush" reconfirms this explicitly** — the answer about his limp ("my brother threw me into a pillar"/callback to the column) is flagged in-world as truthful.
11. **Lucifer is always operating inside deliberate limits on his power while in his body.** He's powerful but beatable in a fight — the explicit, bible-locked reason Azrael can genuinely hurt him.
12. **Gabriel handles God's physical arrangements on Earth** — the explanatory mechanism any future "someone already took care of X before Lucifer could" beat should use.
13. **Lucifer knows general human desire** (money, wants, how phones/institutions work) from ruling Hell — never a read on a specific individual.
14. **Lucifer does not carry his own phone, but physically operates the shared one.** Azrael owns/carries no working fluency with it; Lucifer is the one who unlocks it, works the contacts menu, and produces it from his own jacket in both "Errands" and "Ambush" — consistent across both chapters. The phone itself is registered under "Adrian Black" — see § 5 for the still-open naming question.
15. **New, "Ambush": Lucifer's subconscious eye-avoidance of Elizabeth is now an on-page, demonstrated habit** (not yet explained to the reader or to him) — any future scene between them should treat eye contact as a meaningful, trackable variable rather than a neutral default, until the bible catches up with a documented rule (see § 7).

## 9. Pipeline / Process Notes (current as of this rework — check `.claude/agents/agent-generic-director.md`'s Standing Rule before assuming any of this hasn't changed again)

- **Writer's context is deliberately tiny:** the author's short paragraph for the chapter, plus `notes/character-synopsis.md` — nothing else.
- **Revision is a single consolidated agent** doing continuity/voice-drift, soul/style fidelity, and fact-checking in one pass per chapter — findings only, never edits. Its own findings are not always applied before a chapter locks (see § 5's open items) — always check the chapter file and `notes/character-synopsis.md` directly rather than assuming a Revision fix landed.
- **"Ambush" is a concrete demonstration of why this narrow-context trick needs active upkeep:** Revision caught that the synopsis's own "After Ambush" entry had drifted from the actual locked chapter text (the house-purchase reveal). This was corrected same-pass by the Director. Worth remembering going forward: a synopsis entry can go stale the moment a chapter is hand-edited after the entry is first drafted — Revision checking the synopsis against the real chapter text, not just trusting it, is what caught this.
- **No agent — Director included — rewrites or edits a chapter based on review findings.** Findings get reported; the author acts on them (or doesn't, per their own judgment).
- **`chapters/morning.md` is permanently protected.**
- **The repo chapter file is not always the author's final prose** — style-only fixes may live only in the author's personal Doc. Several MINOR/style findings across "Rundown," "No Place Like Home," "Errands," and now "Ambush" (typos, a hedge phrase, bare dialogue tags, the still-unresolved 3+ Speaker Rule stretch in "Ambush") fall into this category and should not be read as unresolved problems requiring action; only CRITICAL/plot-affecting findings flagged in § 5 and § 7 need real attention.
- **Creative Partner** is active for ongoing chapter-planning brainstorming. The "Ambush" planning thread in `notes/creative-partner-session.md` is a useful worked example of the process: the author's outline arrived in rounds (with explicit "more is coming" markers), Creative Partner raised build-out questions only once told the outline was complete, flagged a structural concern (apparent contradiction with "Errands") that turned out to be a misreading on a closer check, and ultimately concluded no chapter-splice was needed at all — the whole confrontation fit cleanly into the new chapter without touching "Errands." Worth remembering: not every apparent cross-chapter contradiction is real; check the actual locked prose before proposing an edit to it.
- **A new session should open by reading this file, then `bible/manuscript-order.md` for the next unwritten chapter's status.** As of this pass, there is no next-chapter paragraph to load yet (§ 2) — check with the author/Creative Partner notes before assigning Writer anything new.
