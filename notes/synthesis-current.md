## 3. Locked Canon (2026-09-11 rework + since)

Full detail lives in the four companion bible docs listed in § 1; this is the load-bearing summary.

- **Lucifer has power from the start of the book.** Not bond-gated. Constrained instead by standing orders: never self-disclose his own divinity (exception: if a human independently figures it out, that's allowed to stand), never reveal his mission, never use power where a human can witness it (passes it off as a magic trick if he must). — *`bible/lucifer_character_profile.md` § "Power & Secrecy"*
- **His baseline personality is volatile** — snide, sarcastic, angry, lashes out at small things — softening in small, informal, unnamed beats over the book, with real acceleration once he falls for Elizabeth. The old named emotional ladder (Numbness→Calm→Beauty/Desire→...) is retired with no formal replacement; a future Revision pass should judge "does this read as before or after he's falling for her," not track a numbered stage. — *`bible/lucifer_character_profile.md` § "Emotional Arc"*
- **He is the original angel** — God gave him his form; every other angel is patterned from his image. This is the (reinforcing, not new) reason he can bond across both light and dark alignments. Not God's offspring — God's most devoted worshipper, because God could create from nothing and no angel could. His own gift was smaller-scale creation (art, music) — the thing God loved him for.
- **God is NOT Lucifer's father.** Their bond reads closer to siblings. Corrected 2026-09-11 across the bible; don't use father-language for God from Lucifer's side.
- **He paints privately from early in the book.** Elizabeth discovers it before she knows the truth about him — her first real evidence he's hurt rather than simply hostile. He sings later, only after she knows, only because she pushes him into it. **Neither beat has happened on the page yet** as of "Introductions Are In Order."
- **Banished from Eden (now relocated to Heaven) and separately barred from Heaven by his own Hell-warden duties** — both existing facts, reframed to explain his resentment of Earth specifically. He does not resent God.
- **Elizabeth genuinely hates him at first.** She stays in his orbit because she's drawn to things she perceives as broken, and can tell he's lashing out at something — not because he's charming. Constant arguing/friction is their default mode; he can't kill her, so there's heat but no real danger. **See § 5 below — this got drafted wrong once and was explicitly corrected.**
- **Azrael's usual death-duty is temporarily reassigned** to a different, unnamed angel (not yet named — open item, § 6). His new job is to contain Lucifer, not evaluate his fitness to bond. He must be present in every Lucifer scene until Elizabeth knows the truth. He and Lucifer physically fight over the book — can genuinely cripple or nearly kill each other, but neither can actually die; both heal within 24 hours. Fights happen mainly at the shared mansion or outside of town. **The mansion has not appeared on the page yet** as of "Introductions Are In Order."
- **Bond system: Travel Bond is the only tier that survives**, same ~20ft tether/range mechanic as before, now purely a proximity mechanic, not a power gate. Power Bond and Full Anchor are cut entirely. Once Elizabeth knows the truth, Lucifer has to stay attached to either her or Azrael. — *`bible/bond_system_reference.md`*
- **Names:** Lucifer → Adrian (on-page origin: "Why Did It Have to Be Apples," his own truth-loophole technique, not an alias assigned to him). Azrael → Tristan. Narration uses the true names (Lucifer/Azrael) regardless of who's present in the scene; only spoken self-introduction and anything a human calls them switches to the alias. Azrael privately calls Lucifer "Lucy" (a real, demonstrated slip-risk even around humans). God calls him "Luc." No one else uses either nickname.
- **Eve is the antagonist, absent from the page until Act Three.** No content or tension from her direction is allowed before then (this predates the rework and still holds per `bible/style-guide.md`).

## 4. Standing Character-Voice Rules (apply to every chapter, not just the one that introduced them)

These live in `notes/character-synopsis.md` under "Standing habits" specifically so Writer — who never reads the bible — still sees them. Treat this section as load-bearing for any new chapter brief.

1. **Elizabeth/Lucifer friction is mandatory, not chemistry (corrected 2026-09-25).** A first draft of "Introductions Are In Order" played their first exchange as warm and easy — comfortable silences, teasing read as attraction, Lucifer registering enjoyment or "liking" something about her, an "I feel exposed" beat, a sincere "so do I." **All of that was wrong and was cut in the redraft.** Locked going forward: his caustic baseline is aimed at her as much as anyone; friction/open arguing is their default mode from the very first exchange, not an occasional beat; he can't kill her so there's real heat but no real danger. She stays in his orbit because she reads him as broken, not because she likes him yet. Concretely: no line where his interiority registers warmth/attraction/liking toward her; no comfortable/companionable silences between the two of them specifically; his teasing/deflection toward her should land as genuinely annoyed or cutting, not charming. This does not mean nonstop cruelty — it means the default temperature between them is cold/adversarial.
2. **Not every character reads people (locked 2026-09-25).** Only two characters in this cast get an analytical read on others: **Elizabeth** knows when someone is being untruthful (that's the whole of it — not general perceptiveness), and **Tristan/Azrael** reads his brother's specific moods and tells (six thousand years of proximity), not people in general. Nobody else — Sophie, James, Claire, Sam, or any town extra — catches subtext, clocks a tell, or reads what someone's not saying. Everyone else is ordinary and a little oblivious by default. — *also locked the same day in `bible/style-guide.md` § 2*
3. **Lucifer eats apples constantly, as a habit (added 2026-09-19).** Ordinary apples, distinct from the Tree of Knowledge fruit thread (see § 6 — that thread closed in "Introductions Are In Order"). He likes them; eating one is how he manages anger/boredom in the moment. Should appear often in mundane/low-stakes scenes, especially early in the book while he's most volatile. **He cannot just set one down once picked up — he has to eat it or destroy it.** A real compulsion, don't explain it on the page.
4. **No similes, no metaphors in narration** (locked 2026-09-05, broadened to cover metaphors 2026-09-11, "permission explicitly revoked" 2026-09-19 per style guide language) — zero exceptions in narration; dialogue is exempt. This is a deliberate floor, not permanent ("I will add some in later" — author's words); no retroactive sweep of earlier chapters is planned until the end of the manuscript. — *`bible/style-guide.md` § "No Similes, No Metaphors in Narration"*
5. **No explicit physical description** of hair color or eye color, for any character, ever. Outfit, facial expression, and generic build/coloring/scars are all fair game. — *`bible/style-guide.md`*
6. **Lucifer is not all-knowing.** He does not get to state something as fact unless he's actually in a position to know it (direct observation this scene, something already disclosed to him on-page, or an ability he's actually used). This has been a recurring drafting failure mode in the old system and remains a live risk. — *`bible/lucifer_character_profile.md` § "He Is Not All-Knowing"*
7. **Divine/telepathic speech formatting:** italics, no quotation marks, for God's voice and any telepathic bond traffic. Standard quotes are reserved for audible speech only.
8. **The Sky-Glance:** Lucifer's silent upward glance at God's too-convenient timing — genuine annoyance, not shared amusement, exists only in his own POV, never noticed by another character. One locked, deliberate exception exists ("Why Did It Have to Be Apples," where he shouts an actual line at God and Azrael comments on it) — a one-time beat, not a new default.

## 9. Pipeline / Process Notes (current as of this rework — check `.claude/agents/agent-generic-director.md`'s Standing Rule before assuming any of this hasn't changed again)

- **Writer's context is deliberately tiny:** the author's short paragraph for the chapter, plus `notes/character-synopsis.md` — nothing else. Not the previous chapter's text, not the bible, not a unit-plan outline, not the next chapter. This is an explicit trial ("we'll see how this works") aimed at preventing character drift; `notes/character-synopsis.md` exists specifically to compensate. Writer updates that file itself after drafting (both a Character Development entry and a Plot Points entry).
- **Revision is a single consolidated agent** (2026-09-11) doing what Continuity Checker, Story Integrity, and Proofreader used to do separately, in one pass per chapter — findings only, never edits. Its context is narrow too: the chapter it's reviewing in full, the bible, and `notes/character-synopsis.md` — not the previous chapter's full text.
- **Retired entirely from this project's pipeline** (agent files remain on disk for reference only): Normalcy, Identity Checker, Line Editor, World Builder, Tonal Calibration, Hedge Remover, Punctuation Checker.
- **No agent — Director included — rewrites or edits a chapter based on review findings.** Findings get reported; the author (or Writer's own revision pass, per author direction) acts on them.
- **`chapters/morning.md` is permanently protected** — no agent may edit it without the author's explicit, in-the-moment approval for that specific edit.
- **The repo chapter file is not always the author's final prose.** Style-only fixes (wording, similes/metaphors, grammar) may be made in the author's personal Doc outside the repo and never synced back — only plot-affecting fixes get repasted into `chapters/<title>.md`. Don't assume a chapter file holds the author's true final wording once it's past drafting; it holds the plot-accurate version, which is what matters for bible/synopsis/sequencing purposes.
- **Creative Partner** is active for ongoing chapter-planning brainstorming — calibrated to push back genuinely, but the author's decision always wins; an override becomes a new mechanic, not a blocked conflict, unless it fully contradicts something already locked.
- **A new session should open by reading this file, then `bible/manuscript-order.md` for the next unwritten chapter's status** — there is currently no next-chapter paragraph prompt to load, since none has been written yet (see § 2).
