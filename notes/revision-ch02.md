## CONTINUITY LEDGER — new details invented (all bible-consistent, none contradicting)

Flagged for the author to ratify or overwrite. None of these are load-bearing enough that changing them would require rewriting the chapter's structure.

1. **Hell runs on administrative "cycles"** with a written ledger of three columns: souls received / in active treatment / released. Column three empty.
2. **Intake figure:** "fourteen thousand and some" per cycle, described as slightly down.
3. **Corridors are numbered.** Nine through forty-one near capacity; forty-two being opened. The oldest corridor (Eve's) is "the first corridor," consistent with the mountain-proximity seniority rule.
4. **Torture rotations** are a countable unit — corridor 22's demons went from three to four full relivings per cycle with no change in outcome (originally written as "per day"; revised, see item 8).
5. **Sebastian's tenure:** eleven centuries as a patroller. Bible does not date him.
6. **Cell door mechanics:** doors sit in a housing; the inner face can be worn; Eve wore a four-inch patch of one lower corner thin over centuries until the whole plate released at once. Blood in that corner, old.
7. **Eve's cell:** designated cell one, first corridor; roughly six by eight feet; rows of marks cut into the back wall at seated height, explicitly *not* a day count and not decoded.
8. ~~Time of escape: four to five days by Hell's reckoning, with Lillith noting "It will not be four days on the other side."~~ **REVISED per author decision (2026-09-02): removed.** Two problems: it re-introduced clock-time (see the Time in Hell rule, item on hours below), and it had Lillith confidently analyzing a destination and a cross-realm time translation for an event with zero precedent — and asserting Eve had already reached Earth, when she hasn't yet. Lillith's dialogue now reports only what the door's physical evidence shows (a forced exit, direction "up," nothing more) and explicitly refuses to speculate further ("I do not have a destination, and I will not invent one for you"). Lucifer's petition to God was correspondingly softened from "routed to Earth" to "escaped through force, direction unconfirmed." This also removes the chapter's cover story for why Eve doesn't surface until Act Three — that gap now needs a reason from whoever plans Act Three, or simply needs no explanation (elapsed time may just be true elapsed time).
9b. **Hourly/daily language removed globally per author decision (2026-09-02):** "extra hours," "four rotations a day," the Tuesday/Thursday patrol reference, and "hourly" patrol logging were all rewritten in cycle/round-based terms, and a Time in Hell rule was added to `bible/hell-mechanics.md` (no hours, no shifts, no named days — demons don't work a 9-to-5).
9. **The petition venue:** the highest room of Lucifer's fortress, ceiling open onto nothing. Addressing God is not a ritual — "There was no office and no queue."
10. ~~Azrael physically enters Hell and waits in the throne room.~~ **REVISED per author decision (2026-09-02): Azrael never leaves Earth** — his death-duty is constant and universal, so he cannot travel to Hell even briefly. The throne-room scene was rewritten: Lucifer departs alone, sensing Azrael's assignment and irritation only through their passive bond. Bible updated to match (`story-bible.md` Realms section, `tristan_azrael_character_profile.md`). Chapter now ends with Lucifer departing Hell alone for Earth, per the corrected `unit-plans.md` Ch.2 ending. Ch.4's plan was also corrected: Azrael is already waiting in the orchard when Lucifer lands, rather than traveling there with him.
11. **Formatting convention:** God's speech and Lucifer's petition are set in italics without quotation marks, to mark them as non-audible. Spoken dialogue keeps standard quotation marks. New to the manuscript.
12. **Lucifer's ring** appears once as a physical object ("gave none of it back") with no inscription quoted — kept cold on purpose. Inscription remains available for a later chapter.
13. **NEW (2026-09-02, author-directed): Lucifer directly sights Eve mid-escape.** He rounds the last turn while she is still kneeling in the wrecked doorway; she looks up, says "Lucifer?", and is pulled away by the same force that freed her before he can reach her. This replaces the earlier staging (Lillith already on scene, forensic-only discovery) with Lucifer discovering the breach alone, witnessing the tail end of the escape himself, and only summoning Lillith afterward — she now arrives *after* being called through the bond ("First corridor. Now."), not before. His reaction is the requested register: not personal, but a flicker of procedural dread — he'll have to report this to God, and that's what the moment costs him, explicitly not her name or history. His petition to God was updated accordingly, from "direction unconfirmed" to reporting the sighting directly ("I saw it happen... pursue it on Earth").
14. **Register check re-sequenced:** now happens because Lucifer chooses to formally confirm what he already knows (he heard her voice), not as the reader's reveal mechanism — a small character beat (procedure even when personally unnecessary) rather than a plot device.
