CATEGORY: Continuity
SEVERITY: CRITICAL
LOCATION: "Beside him, Azrael was still fiddling with the phone. He didn't say anything. He just giggled to himself knowingly. / 'Gabriel,' he whispered forcefully, low enough that it didn't carry past his own jaw, and with real feeling behind it. / Denise didn't catch it. 'Sorry, what was that?'"
ISSUE: Three stacked problems in this one beat. (1) It is Azrael, not Lucifer, who identifies and names Gabriel here. Every other authoritative record of this exact chapter — `bible/gabriel_character_profile.md` ("Lucifer knows at once it was Gabriel"), `bible/story-bible.md`'s GABRIEL section ("Lucifer knows at once it was Gabriel"), and `notes/character-synopsis.md`'s own "After Errands" Plot Points ("Lucifer immediately knows this is Gabriel's doing... and curses him under his breath; Denise doesn't catch any of it. Azrael goes still... noticing Lucifer has clocked something, but says nothing about it in the room") — all agree the realization and the muttered curse belong to Lucifer, with Azrael picking up on it silently via the brothers' mutual-read trait and saying nothing aloud until they're outside. The chapter as written reverses this: Lucifer never privately names Gabriel at all (on the page his reaction is only "understood... that it very much could be" — he never completes the thought), and it's Azrael who says the name aloud. (2) It's unclear how Azrael would independently arrive at "Gabriel" with no on-page basis for his own realization, since the POV is Lucifer's — this reads as information Azrael has no stated way of possessing yet (echoes the "He is not all-knowing" epistemic rule, applied here to Azrael rather than Lucifer, but the same failure mode). (3) "Denise didn't catch it" directly contradicts her very next line, "Sorry, what was that?" — a direct, immediate self-contradiction within the same short passage. If she didn't catch it, she has no reason to ask what it was.
SUGGESTED FIX: Reassign the realization and the muttered name to Lucifer's own POV (a private, silent "Gabriel" landing in his thoughts, or a low curse under his own breath) per the locked record, with Azrael's reaction limited to going still / clocking that something's wrong without saying the name aloud in front of Denise. Resolve the "didn't catch it" / "what was that" contradiction either by having her genuinely not react at all, or by having her react to something actually audible (and then the "didn't catch it" line needs to go).

WHAT'S WORKING

- The seam lands exactly where the manuscript order says it should: "I think you broke my back" as the opening line, Lucifer favoring his back and limping throughout without Azrael showing any injury, is a clean, well-threaded continuation of "No Place Like Home" and the "only Lucifer is hurt" planted detail.
- The bribery scene is a genuinely good on-page test of two brand-new, author-locked cosmology rules at once: "Deals aren't sins" (`bible/lucifer_character_profile.md` § POWER & SECRECY) and the new "general knowledge of human desire, never individual-specific" rule — "Lucifer wasn't guessing at anything specific — he didn't need to. Every human he'd ever dealt with wanted the same handful of things, and money solved more of them than people liked to admit." This is explicitly the corrected version (per the synopsis's own note about cutting an earlier draft that had him read a private detail about Kevin's daughter), and the fix lands cleanly — institutional knowledge, not individual perception.
- Lucifer's volatile baseline voice is consistent throughout — short, impatient, dryly superior ("Just a phone... Obviously," "Don't you dare make me regret this later") — without tipping into warmth or softness that wouldn't be earned yet, and without melodrama.
- "I don't like phones. That doesn't mean I don't know how to use them," paired with the quiet internal line about six thousand years running a domain built on what people want, is an efficient, non-expository way to reinforce his competence without making him omniscient — good compliance with the "he is not all-knowing" epistemic rule even while showing real mastery.
