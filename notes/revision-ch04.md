## RESOLVED SEQUENCING QUESTION

`bible/unit-plans.md`'s bullet list and `bible/jack_and_claire_character_profile.md` implied two different orders for the ring:

- **unit-plans.md** lists "Lucifer notices James's ring and replaces it" *before* the Travel Bond bullet, which read like the ring gets fixed early, using some kind of pre-bond or Travel-Bond-tier transfiguration.
- **jack_and_claire_character_profile.md** is explicit: "This disclosure immediately upgrades both to Power Bond, which is what permits the small Hell conjuring used to replace James's ring." That only works if the ring is fixed *after* disclosure.

The bond system in `bible/story-bible.md` (Stage 2 Travel Bond = "no power," Stage 3 Power Bond = unlocks small transfigurations) only supports the character profile's version — a pre-Power-Bond ring fix would be a hard contradiction. I went with **notice early, act late**: Lucifer clocks the ring's flaw during the picnic (satisfying the "notices" beat and the permitted aesthetic flicker) but doesn't touch it until after the Power Bond forms at the end, consistent with both the bond mechanics and the character profile. unit-plans.md's bullet order was just a list, not a strict timeline, so I don't think this needs a bible edit — flagging in case the author intended something else.

## AUTHOR-DIRECTED CHANGE (2026-09-02): Violent landing + finger-point at God

Replaced the original quiet arrival ("the portal did not release him so much as stop holding him... he came up already standing") with the author's request: the portal throws him hard enough to hit an apple tree, he gets up, and points a finger at the sky ("That was unnecessary").

This merges two things that were previously separate beats:
- The rough, involuntary nature of the landing (already established at the end of Ch.2).
- The chapter's required sky-glance moment (Lucifer clocks the orchard/symbolism, looks up, Azrael doesn't understand it) — now delivered as the same gesture, just more overt (pointing, one line of dialogue) than the style guide's usual silent single glance.

I did **not** keep a second, separate quiet sky-glance later in the chapter (the original draft had one at a second falling apple) — using the device twice in one chapter would spend most of its whole-book budget (2-3 uses total) immediately. `bible/unit-plans.md` Ch.4 updated to document this as the chapter's one sky-glance use, and its Must-NOT line softened with a narrow exception for this specific beat (dry exasperation at God, not an emotional reaction to anything on Earth) rather than reopening "no emotion beyond mild efficiency" for the rest of the chapter.

## AUTHOR-DIRECTED CHANGE (2026-09-02): Lucifer more brooding, not bantering with Tristan

The briefing exchange originally had Lucifer trading dry wit with Azrael ("That is remarkably unhelpful" / "It's remarkably effective, different thing"; "You've reminded me zero times, if we're counting" / "I'm counting now") — too warm and engaged for the numbness phase he's in pre-Calm-unlock. Rewrote so Lucifer is curt, disengaged, and doesn't push back playfully — he asks what he needs to and disengages rather than sparring. Azrael's annoyance at this now shows only physically (his jaw going tight, twice) and is explicitly never remarked on by either of them, per the author's direction that Tristan is annoyed but won't say so. Applies as a standing calibration for Lucifer's voice in all scenes until Calm unlocks, not just this one.

## AUTHOR-DIRECTED ADDITION (2026-09-02): Bond argument before disclosure

Added a short telepathic exchange right before Lucifer discloses his divinity to James and Claire: Azrael warns him not to confess so easily ("you don't get to take it back once it's said"), Lucifer pushes back twice, and Azrael has no reply — Lucifer proceeds with the disclosure anyway. Kept it cold and clipped rather than a real back-and-forth, consistent with the no-banter-before-Calm-unlocks rule locked in earlier — this is a genuine disagreement about strategy, not wit, and Lucifer "wins" by simply acting rather than by out-arguing him.

## UPDATE (2026-09-04) — Intensified James and Claire's reaction to the disclosure

Author's direction: humans learning the brothers are celestial beings should register as a genuine, brief system-shock — "physically break their brain for a few moments" — not just banter-adjacent disbelief. The scene already had good escalation (getting up and sitting back down, the water glass stalling, "I need a minute too"), but nothing captured the first instant of impact. Added a paragraph immediately after "We're angels" showing both of them momentarily stalled — James's mouth open on a word that doesn't arrive, Claire's hand gripping the blanket without her having decided to reach for it, neither blinking normally — before the existing recovery beats (the water glass, the unconvincing laugh) pick back up. Kept everything externally observable, since this is Lucifer's POV and he has no access to their interior sensations — no claims about what James or Claire are feeling inside, only what Lucifer can see happening to them. No hedge phrases or similes introduced; checked against the style guide.
